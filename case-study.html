01 - The Problem

Customers could not quickly understand what the business offered.

[Client type] needed a clearer website because visitors were struggling to find the most important details: services, pricing or menu, location, opening hours, contact options and proof of trust.

On mobile, the existing experience required too much hunting. Key calls to action were buried, screenshots carried text that search engines could not read, and the page hierarchy did not support fast decision-making.

02 - The Solution

I rebuilt the page around a 30-second evaluation path.

The new structure prioritized the information a visitor needs first: what the business does, who it is for, what action to take next, why the visitor should trust it, and how the page supports search or acquisition intent.

  • Created a responsive layout with clear section hierarchy and generous whitespace.
  • Moved important copy into selectable HTML text instead of flattening it into images.
  • Designed interactive device mockups so stakeholders could review the live experience, not only static screenshots.
  • Improved growth basics such as headings, local intent, CTA clarity, and metadata-ready copy.
  • Prepared async documentation for decisions, revisions and launch notes.

The goal was not to make the website louder. The goal was to make the offer easier to understand and easier to act on.

03 - The Output & Business Impact

A clearer, faster and more credible web presence.

The final output was a responsive static website with reusable project sections, stronger mobile calls to action, semantic case study content and a lower-maintenance frontend stack.
